    How the dish works

    Physarum polycephalum is a slime mold. Tubes carry its protoplasm between the oat flakes. A tube that carries a lot grows thicker, and one that carries little withers.

    Outside the city the dish is lit, the way the researchers lit the sea and the mountains around Tokyo. Places more than 500 m from any bus, tram or train stop get a dimmer light. The slime can cross them but would rather not.
